Privacy Policy for Keymage

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Keymage ("the app") is an offline password generator and manager developed by Sepehr Amini. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your data when you use the app.

Summary

Keymage does not collect, transmit, or share any of your personal data. Everything the app creates or stores lives only on your own device. The app does not use the internet for anything except opening links you explicitly tap (such as this privacy policy or the developer's website).

Data the app does not collect

  • No account or sign-up — the app has no login, no user profile, and no server backend.
  • No analytics or usage tracking of any kind.
  • No advertising and no ad-tracking identifiers.
  • No cloud sync — your data is never uploaded anywhere by the app.
  • No access to your contacts, location, microphone, or camera.

Data stored on your device

Everything below is stored locally, in the app's private storage area, and is never sent anywhere:

  • Your saved passwords and hints — kept in a local database on your device, used only to display your password list inside the app.
  • Your app password (if you choose to protect the app with a custom password instead of your device's screen lock) — stored locally in a hashed (irreversible) form, never in plain text and never transmitted.
  • Your "secret image" (optional recovery method) — when you pick an image from your gallery to use as a backup login method, the app reads that image only to compute a cryptographic fingerprint (a SHA-256 hash) of it. Only this fingerprint is stored on your device. The image itself is never copied, stored, or uploaded by the app — it stays exactly where it already was in your device's gallery.

Permissions the app uses, and why

  • Photo / gallery access — used solely to let you pick the secret image described above. The app processes the image on your device and does not retain or share it.
  • Storage access — used only when you choose to export or import your password vault. Exporting creates an encrypted backup file on your own device (in your Downloads folder); importing reads a backup file you select. Keymage never uploads this file anywhere — what you do with it afterward (e.g. moving it to your own cloud storage) is entirely up to you.
  • Biometric / device screen-lock authentication — if you choose to unlock the app using your device's fingerprint, face unlock, PIN, or pattern, this is handled entirely by Android's own operating-system security APIs. Keymage never sees, collects, or stores your biometric data — it only receives a yes/no "authentication succeeded" result from Android.

Third parties

Keymage does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection SDKs. The only outbound actions the app can take are ones you explicitly trigger yourself: using the "Share app" option (which hands off to Android's normal share sheet, letting you choose where to send it) or tapping the developer's website / this privacy policy link, which opens in your browser.

Children's privacy

Keymage is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, regardless of age — since it does not collect personal data from any user.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated occasionally, for example if new features change how data is handled. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
